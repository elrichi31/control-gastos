# Landing pública

Scope: only src/app/page.tsx and src/components/landing. Mode: Persuade. Dashboard, authentication pages and internal tools retain their visual system.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Show BethaSpend as a straightforward personal expense tool, replacing particle effects and rotating copy with an immediately legible offer.
OWN-WORLD: User-pinned expo.dev reference: neutral canvas, dark typography, pill actions, full-width quiet navigation, generous whitespace. Retain BethaSpend wallet mark and restrained green for financial status; reuse the existing Inter font, also observed on the reference.
STORY: Understand expenses and budgets, see an illustrative monthly ledger, then register through the existing public flow.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned static heading and actions; right-hand rendered ledger with clearly marked sample data. Mobile stacks copy before ledger. No fake customer logos, security absolutes or invented product capabilities.
FORM: Pinned reference, no concept seed; code-led refinement of the existing landing only. Interaction: accessible mobile navigation, anchor links and registration actions. No perpetual animation.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

No new raster assets. Existing product facts and actions are retained, with redundant marketing removed. Desktop and mobile, light and dark are required verification targets.
