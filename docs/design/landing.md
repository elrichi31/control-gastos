# Landing pública

Scope: only homepage and src/components/landing. Mode: Persuade. Never change dashboard, auth or email review.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Make the expense workflow memorable through a live, clearly illustrative product stage; avoid an icon grid dressed as a redesign.
OWN-WORLD: User-pinned Expo screenshots: charcoal canvas, large rounded panels, oversized white Inter typography, layered UI objects, directional lighting and restrained green/blue highlights. Preserve BethaSpend identity and theme support.
STORY: See a sample expense enter the ledger and update its budget; explore dedicated budget, recurring and category scenes; register through existing links.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered, static oversized heading, pill actions, then a wide animated product stage with a central phone, floating expense form and category chip. Mobile stacks the scene without shrinking readable UI. All financial values are labeled illustrative.
FORM: User-pinned composition, code-led. No random direction selection. Finite expense demo, replay button, scroll entrances, subtle scene motion only in view; global pause, reduced-motion and background-tab controls. Large asymmetric panels, typographic interlude, immersive rounded closing CTA.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

No invented adoption metrics, logos, commercial claims or new dependencies. No shipping raster assets; the stage is semantic, runtime-rendered sample product UI, not a copied Expo illustration. Validate actual hydrated components, desktop/mobile, themes and motion controls. Existing static-preview limitations do not count as interaction verification.
